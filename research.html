i. Higher-Dimensional Concepts in LLMs
How LLMs Follow Instructions
Developed a diagnostic framework (probing, INLP, causal ablation, PWCCA) showing that instruction-following in LLMs works through compositional coordination of skills rather than a single universal mechanism.
How LLMs Follow Instructions (EMNLP 2026) how-llms-follow-instructions diagnostic probing INLP / causal ablation PWCCA
Counting & Length Control
Investigated how LLMs internally track and enforce word-count constraints, and how instruction-tuning imparts this ability across languages, using Direct Logit Attribution and model diffing.
How Instruction-Tuning Imparts Length Control (ANNPR 2026) length-control clt-dataset Direct Logit Attribution model diffing
Refusal: Diff-in-Means vs INLP
Reproduced and extended work showing refusal is mediated by a single direction in activation space, comparing difference-in-means with INLP to test whether refusal is better described by a higher-dimensional subspace. Erasing the refusal subspace leaves activations between the harmful and harmless clusters, while reflecting across it moves them to the opposite cluster: tentative evidence that models encode the absence of a concept differently from its opposite. The project started in the BlueDot Impact Technical AI Safety Project Sprint.
Refusal Beyond a Single Direction (2026) Extracting Refusal Direction: Diff-in-means vs INLP refusal_direction activation steering
ii. Geometric Approaches
Exploring Input Manifolds of Transformers
Studying equivalence classes in Transformer input spaces with Riemannian geometry and pullback metrics, to reveal which inputs a model perceives as the same. Presented at NeurIPS 2025 and the UniReps workshop.
Unveiling Transformer Perception (NeurIPS 2025) transformers_equivalence_classes pullback metrics
Riemannian Manifold Steering
Using Riemannian geometry to steer language models along concept manifolds. Currently under review.
Riemannian Manifold Steering (under review) Riemannian geometry activation steering
iii. Learning Dynamics
Transformers as Complex Networks
Representing Transformer LLMs as directed, weighted graphs (nodes = attention heads and MLPs, edges = causal influence measured by ablation), and tracking graph-theoretic metrics across 143 training checkpoints of Pythia-14M on induction tasks.
Modeling Transformers as Complex Networks (2025) nnets-transformers Complex Network Theory
