AI Researcher · AREA Science Park

Elisabetta
Rocchetti

“Don't Panic.” — The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

I'm a researcher at the Laboratory of Data Engineering (LADE), AREA Science Park, and President of AI Safety for Italy. I work on mechanistic interpretability and representation geometry: how models work internally and how they structure what they know. My recent work covers how LLMs follow instructions, multi-dimensional concept steering, and the manifold structure and training dynamics of deep neural networks, with a focus on Transformers. I've also studied how text-to-image models interpret artistic style, and I'm particularly interested in how models represent complex, abstract concepts.
